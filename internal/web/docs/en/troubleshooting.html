Troubleshooting and FAQ

I forgot my master password

Use your recovery key: Forgot the master password? on the sign-in page. Without a recovery key the vault cannot be opened by anyone; ask your administrator to delete the account and invite you again.

I lost my phone with the authenticator app

Sign in with one of your recovery codes instead of the app code. Without codes, an administrator resets your second factor; your vault is not affected.

"Too many failed attempts"

Your address was blocked after failed sign-ins. Wait until the time shown, or ask an administrator to release it (Settings → Sign-in and security, or passzit-server --unblock-ip ADDRESS on the server).

The vault locks too often

It locks after the inactivity time of the policy. Administrators can raise it under Settings → Vault policy; users can only choose a shorter time.

Unlocking takes a few seconds

That is the key derivation working as intended — it makes guessing master passwords slow. On old PCs and phones, an administrator can lower the memory in the policy (not below 19 MiB).

"This was changed on another device in the meantime"

The item was saved elsewhere while you edited it. Click Reload and make your change again.

"This item cannot be decrypted"

The stored data is damaged or was changed outside PassZit. The authenticated encryption refuses to show changed data. Restore the item from a backup of the database.

The page says it needs JavaScript

All encryption happens in the browser, so PassZit cannot work without JavaScript. Allow scripts for the PassZit address.
