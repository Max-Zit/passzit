Secret links and emergency access

Give one person a password without an account, let trusted people into your vault when something happens to you, and see who used an item of a shared vault.

Secret links

Tools → Secret links (or Send a copy in an item) makes a link for a text or a file up to 8 MB. Choose how long it works (1 hour to 30 days) and how often it can be opened (1–100 times; once is the default). The content is encrypted in your browser; the key is the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to a server. The recipient opens the link in any browser, no account needed; after the last allowed opening the server deletes the encrypted data.

An optional password protects the link a second time: the content cannot be decrypted without it, and wrong guesses are counted and blocked like failed sign-ins. Tell the password on another way than the link (phone, another messenger).

The list under Your links shows how often each link was opened and when it ends; Delete stops it at once. The policy can switch secret links off (Settings → Vault policy → Sharing and export). Recipients outside your networks need to reach the server: check Settings → Network and access.

Emergency access

Under My account → Emergency access you name up to five people who may open your personal vault if something happens to you, each with a waiting time (0–90 days, 7 by default). Your browser seals your vault key to their public key; the server keeps it sealed.

  1. The person asks for access on their My account page.
  2. You see the request on your My account page and can Reject it (or Approve now).
  3. Without your answer the access opens after the waiting time. The person can then read your personal vault (read only) in their browser. Every step is in the audit log.

Removing the person ends it at once. Shared vaults are not part of it — they have their own members.

Who used an item

In shared vaults the web vault and the extension report when someone shows a password, copies a secret or fills a login. Managers of the vault see this under Who used this item in the item. Reports come from the browsers: someone who changes their software can avoid them, so the log helps against mistakes and answers "who had it", it is no proof.
