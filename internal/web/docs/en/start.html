Getting started

PassZit is a self-hosted password manager. Every user has an encrypted vault for logins and secure notes; everything is encrypted and decrypted in the user's own browser, so the server — and anyone who steals it — never sees a password.

What PassZit does

AreaWhat you get
VaultLogins (user name, password, websites), secure notes, own fields, folders, favourites, search, trash and password history. Working with the vault
GeneratorRandom passwords and easy-to-type passphrases, made in your browser.
SecurityEnd-to-end encryption with a master password only you know, mandatory two-factor sign-in, a recovery key, automatic locking. How PassZit protects your passwords
AdministrationAccounts only by invitation, roles, a tamper-evident audit log, policies for locking, the clipboard and key derivation. Users, invitations and settings
Browser extensionsChrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, Vivaldi and Firefox fill and save logins (coming with version 0.2).

First steps

  1. Install the server and open its address in a browser (Installing the server).
  2. Set up PassZit: enter the setup token from the server's data directory, your e-mail address and a master password. The browser makes your keys — this takes a second.
  3. Set up two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app and keep the recovery codes.
  4. Make a recovery key and store it offline. It is the only way back if you forget the master password.
  5. Add your first login with New item, and invite your colleagues under Users.

Remember the master password. PassZit never knows it. Nobody — not the administrator, not MaxZit — can open your vault without the master password or your recovery key.

The screens

  • Vault — your items. On the left the filters (all, favourites, types, folders, trash), in the middle the list with search, on the right the selected item.
  • Password generator — make a password without saving it.
  • My account — name, lock and clipboard times, master password, recovery key, recovery codes and your sessions.
  • Users, Audit log, Settings — for administrators.

The Lock button at the top locks the vault at once; it also locks by itself after the inactivity time your administrator chose (15 minutes by default).
