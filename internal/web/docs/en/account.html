Master password, recovery key and two-factor sign-in

Everything about your own account is under My account.

Choosing a master password

The master password protects everything else, so it should be long and only used here. A passphrase of four to six random words (for example from the generator) is easy to remember and hard to guess. The meter shows an estimate; the policy sets a minimum length (12 characters by default).

Changing the master password

Enter the current and the new master password. Your browser wraps your account key again with the new password; the items themselves stay as they are, so this is quick. Your other sessions are signed out.

Recovery key

Make a recovery key shows a key of 32 letters and digits — only once. Print it or write it down and keep it offline, for example with your important documents. Making a new key replaces the old one; you can also remove it.

If you forget the master password: on the sign-in page click Forgot the master password?, enter your e-mail address, the recovery key, a code of your authenticator app and a new master password.

Anyone with your recovery key and your second factor can take over your vault. Do not store the recovery key in the vault itself or next to your phone.

Two-factor sign-in

At the first sign-in you scan a QR code with an authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, Authy, 2FAS, …) and get ten recovery codes. Each code replaces the app once. With a code of the app you can make new recovery codes; the old ones stop working.

Lost the phone and the codes? An administrator can reset your second factor; you set it up again at the next sign-in. Your vault is not affected.

Security keys and passkeys

Under Security keys and passkeys you can add a security key (YubiKey, Titan …) or a passkey of your device (Windows Hello, Touch ID, Android). At sign-in, after the master password, Use a security key or passkey replaces the code of the authenticator app. The app and the recovery codes stay as a fallback, and browser extensions still use the app. Keys work only on the HTTPS address they were added on.

Your key for sharing

The fingerprint of your public key, computed in your browser. People who share a vault with you see the same fingerprint; compare it once with them.

Locking and the clipboard

You can choose a shorter lock time and a shorter clipboard time than the policy allows. Empty fields use the policy's values.

Sessions

The list shows where you are signed in. Sign out all other sessions ends all except this one.
